企鵝冰橋 Pengu Bridge 隱私權政策

開發者：Suliba ・ 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

本政策說明 Android 遊戲「企鵝冰橋 Pengu Bridge」（套件名稱 com.sulibaa.pengubridge，以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理你的資料。

1. 我們自己不收集個人資料

本遊戲不需要註冊或登入，也沒有自己的伺服器。下列遊戲資料只存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者：

  • 關卡進度、每日挑戰紀錄、提示次數與小魚乾數量
  • 音樂、音效、震動、語言等設定
  • 是否已購買「移除廣告」

這些資料以加密檔案儲存在本遊戲的私有資料夾，解除安裝本遊戲時會一併刪除。

2. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 是 Google 提供的第三方服務，為了顯示廣告、衡量成效、數據分析及防範詐欺，可能會自動收集並與 Google 分享以下資料：

  • 裝置識別碼，例如廣告 ID
  • 由 IP 位址推估的大概位置
  • 應用程式互動資料，例如廣告的顯示與點擊
  • 當機紀錄、診斷資料及其他應用程式效能資料

資料在傳輸過程中會加密。Google 如何使用這些資料，請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊 及 Google 隱私權政策。

你可以在 Android 的「設定 → Google → 廣告」（或「設定 → 隱私權 → 廣告」）重設或刪除廣告 ID，或選擇不接收個人化廣告。購買「移除廣告」後，本遊戲不再顯示插頁廣告。

3. 應用程式內購（Google Play 結帳）

「移除廣告」與提示包等應用程式內購都透過 Google Play 結帳系統處理。付款資訊由 Google 收集與處理，開發者不會取得你的信用卡或其他付款資料。詳見 Google 付款隱私權聲明。

4. 兒童

本遊戲的目標對象為 13 歲以上的使用者，並非專為兒童設計，我們也不會在知情的情況下收集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資料。

5. 刪除資料

遊戲資料只存在你的裝置上，解除安裝本遊戲即可全部刪除。開發者沒有保存你的任何資料，所以無法也不需要另外提供刪除服務。由 Google 收集的廣告相關資料，請依上方第 2 點的方式管理。

6. 政策更新

本政策更新時，會修改此頁面並更新上方的生效日期。

7. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信至 suliba.dev@gmail.com。

Pengu Bridge Privacy Policy

Developer: Suliba · Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game "Pengu Bridge" (package name com.sulibaa.pengubridge, "the Game") handles your data.

1. We do not collect personal data ourselves

The Game has no sign-up or login and no servers of its own. The following game data stays on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Level progress, daily challenge records, hint counts and fish collected
  • Settings such as music, sound effects, vibration and language
  • Whether you have purchased "Remove Ads"

This data is stored in an encrypted file in the Game's private folder and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, a third-party service provided by Google. To serve ads, measure their performance, run analytics and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect the following data and share it with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and clicks
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and other app performance data

This data is encrypted in transit. To learn how Google uses it, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). After you buy "Remove Ads", the Game no longer shows interstitial ads.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

"Remove Ads", hint packs and other in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment information is collected and processed by Google; the developer never receives your card or other payment details. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Deleting your data

Game data exists only on your device; uninstalling the Game deletes all of it. The developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing further to delete on our side. Advertising data collected by Google can be managed as described in section 2.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email suliba.dev@gmail.com.
